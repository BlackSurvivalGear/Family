Egba Land Heritage

The Cradle of Abeokuta

Our historical timeline originates in Abeokuta, nestled underneath the shadow of the historic Olumo Rock in Ogun State, southwestern Nigeria. Established in the early 1800s as a sanctuary of refuge, Abeokuta bred a lineage of fierce intellectual independence, trading industry, and legal excellence.

It is from these Egba roots that our founding patriarch, Alhaji Kolawole Lawal, acquired his passion for structural sciences, paving the path of corporate enterprise and civil development that solidifies the Lawal dynasty.
