Egba Land Heritage

The Cradle of Abeokuta

Our historical timeline originates in Abeokuta, nestled underneath the shadow of the historic Olumo Rock in Ogun State, southwestern Nigeria. Established in the early 1800s as a sanctuary of refuge, Abeokuta bred a lineage of fierce intellectual independence, trading industry, and legal excellence.

It is from these Egba roots that our founding patriarch, Alhaji Kolawole Lawal, acquired his passion for structural sciences, paving the path of corporate enterprise and civil development that solidifies the Lawal dynasty.

Ancestral Homestead Abeokuta, Ogun State, Nigeria
Lagos Consolidation

The Commercial Hub

In the 1970s, the family relocated its administrative core to Lagos, establishing primary businesses in textile, legal practices (SAN), and real estate development.

London Transatlantic

Academic & Medical Excellence

By the late 80s, descendants established roots in the United Kingdom, studying at Oxford, London, and launching leading clinical medical programs at GOSH Hospital.

USA & Capital Expansion

The Next Frontier

Our third-generation members study and lead in the United States, driving venture capital and impact funding to support sustainable infrastructures globally.
